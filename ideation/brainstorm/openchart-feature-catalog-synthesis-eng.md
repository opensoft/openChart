## Frappe realization

- **Core model:** OC-prefixed portal account, request, publication, consent, proxy, communication, integration, task, and audit-projection DocTypes use Links, child tables, private attachments, effective versions, and succession-based amendments for accepted evidence.
- **Portal surfaces:** Frappe Web Forms and portal pages under `www/engagement/` are styled through Website Theme with shared navigation, active-patient context, language, accessibility, status, error, and receipt components; desk workspaces remain unavailable to portal roles.
- **Identity and permissions:** Frappe `Patient` and `Proxy User` roles use owner and explicit-scope permissions, while clinic User Permissions and permlevels constrain desk reviewers; OAuth2/OIDC sessions, passkeys, step-up checks, and app scopes are revalidated on every protected operation.
- **Workflow and assignments:** Frappe Workflows govern review, release, consent, proxy, request, and expiry states; Assignment Rules, Kanban, Query Reports, Number Cards, and review Notifications make exceptions accountable.
- **Communication:** Frappe Email Notifications, SMS Notifications through SMS Settings, Notification Log, realtime events, and RQ jobs apply effective channel consent, language, quiet hours, minimum-necessary content, deduplication, and delivery reconciliation.
- **API and audit:** Guarded `open_chart.api.v1.engagement` whitelisted methods are the supported write surface; hooks reject direct clinical writes and emit immutable audit events for protected views, attempts, transitions, overrides, exports, proxy capacity, and external callbacks.

## Boundaries

Owns: portal identity bindings, engagement requests, patient-facing projections, communication threads and preferences, consent and proxy access state, portal task state, and access evidence. Consumes: authoritative clinical records and release decisions, patient identity, scheduling authority, legal authority review, financial-system projections, content review, integration outcomes, and clinic policy. Emits: governed requests, patient-reported inputs, proxy decisions, notifications, exports, staff assignments, and clinical audit events. Does not own: diagnoses, orders, prescriptions, encounter documentation, scheduling inventory, financial ledger, payment processing, research enrollment, legal adjudication, or autonomous clinical action.

## Emergent behavior

The domain forms a patient-controlled engagement loop: secure enrollment binds a person to a portal identity; release and segmentation rules project only authorized information; requests, forms, tasks, and messages return patient input to accountable desk queues; consent, proxy, and connected-app controls determine who may act and what they may see; and every channel, download, denial, override, and state change feeds one clinical audit spine. Because shared contracts are rechecked at action time, revoking a proxy or OAuth2 grant can immediately narrow portal pages, exports, notifications, device sync, and multi-patient switching without rewriting the underlying clinical record.

## Tensions to hold

- Fast self-service must not bypass identity proofing, staff adjudication, lawful release holds, proxy segmentation, or accepted-record succession.
- Patient-friendly explanations and AI-assisted staff drafts improve comprehension and throughput, but must remain labeled, reviewed, attributable, and non-diagnostic.
- Useful notifications require timely context, while email, SMS, shared devices, kiosks, and external apps demand minimum-necessary disclosure and revocable authorization.
- Family and caregiver access improves continuity, while adolescent confidentiality, custody disputes, age-of-majority transitions, and emergency access require fail-closed policy.
- Engagement analytics can improve access, but outreach response, satisfaction, device, and research-interest data must not become hidden clinical or eligibility judgments.

## Recombination opportunities

- Combine profile changes, uploaded evidence, forms, consent signatures, and virtual arrival into a reusable pre-visit completion journey with one receipt and separate downstream authorities.
- Combine release rules, explanations, trends, reading-level views, decision aids, and question tasks into a comprehension pathway that never delays required access.
- Combine proxy grants, segmentation, multi-patient context, age transitions, revocation, and authority evidence into a single effective-access evaluator used by portal pages and OAuth2 APIs.
- Combine notification preferences, language, accessibility, quiet hours, SMS threads, and campaign consent into a channel-policy service shared by every engagement feature.
- Combine remote measurements, wearable grants, patient tasks, PROMs, and care-plan views into reviewed patient-reported progress without autonomous treatment changes.
